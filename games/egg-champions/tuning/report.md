## Design gaps found

- Coral Coast: the gym is a hard wall. The reference team wins 0% one rebirth below the target and 82% at the target. Damage only varies by the damage roll, so there is almost no luck in a battle.
- Green Meadow: only 2 distinct Rare pets can be hatched by this world. The reference team repeats the best one.
- Ember Volcano: no Rare pet of the counter type (water) exists by this world. The reference team has no type advantage, so this gym is tuned weaker than the design intends.
- Every Rare pet is a Fighter. Rare teams earn half the Click Power budget, and the gyms are tuned against the best battle role.

## Assumptions the spec does not define

1. Leader tuning first calibrates the reference team to the baseline win-rate target, then multiplies that power by the spec's cohort_power_factor (default 1). Reported reference win rates measure the final adjusted leaders, not the baseline. Cohort pacing and rebirth targets remain unchanged.
2. Leader pets: base HP and Attack of the world's most expensive egg, times the strength rarity battle multiplier, times the tuned power. HP and Attack are rounded.
3. Leader pets have Speed 11 (midpoint of speed_min and speed_max) and the Smash special. All pets of a leader team are identical. Leaders get no rebirth or variant bonus.
4. Every special is ready on turn 1. After use it is locked for special_cooldown_turns turns, so it comes back on the third turn after.
5. Turn order is fixed at the start of a turn from current Speed. A pet that faints before acting loses its action. The replacement acts from the next turn. Cooldowns only tick for the active pet.
6. Guard halves the next hit taken, whenever it comes. Rush can stack if used again, but the player policy uses it once.
7. Drain heals from damage dealt, capped at max HP. Heal is capped at max HP.
8. Off-type uses the listed type for the type bonus. Same type against same type is neutral (1.0).
9. Player battle policy: use an attack special (Smash, Drain) when ready. Use Off-type when it beats the basic move after type bonus. Use Heal when ready and HP is under 60%. Use Guard on the turn before the leader's Smash lands. Use Rush once, only when slower than the active leader pet. Otherwise basic attack. Team order is strongest matchup first.
10. Reference team: the 3 distinct Rare pets hatchable by that world with the best matchup score (HP x Attack x own type bonus / leader type bonus). If fewer than 3 distinct Rare pets exist, copies of the best one fill the team. No variants.
11. Clicks are continuous: coins arrive at clicks per second x coins per click. The index bonus multiplies coins per click: x (1 + index_egg_bonus x completed eggs). An egg is complete when all 5 normal pets were hatched. World/full normal collection factors are included when configured. The index survives rebirth. Optional wild focus wins, their coin rewards/temporary boosts and captures are excluded: this is a passive-training baseline, not a forecast for active minigame players.
12. Shiny and Golden are exclusive rolls on each hatch. Their click and battle multipliers apply on top of rarity and role.
13. Hatching takes 3 s and a battle takes 6 s per turn. Legacy clicking pauses during both. Configured training with no hatch pause continues earning during the reveal, using the equipped team before that hatch, and excludes hatch opportunity cost from purchase decisions. Battles still pause income. Gym retry waits earn normally.
14. No game passes. Inventory retains up to team_size copies of each pet/variant (extra identical copies cannot improve a team). Inventory-relative power floors are retired (zero in current config); all pet powers are fixed by pet and variant. Farm teams reselect the best effective coin pets after hatches. Battle training still uses a greedy replacement policy.
15. Greedy player policy, farm mode: buy the unlocked egg with the best expected click gain per coin, as long as the expected gain pays back the price plus the coins lost while hatching within 300 s, or within the time it would take to save the gym fee if that is longer. A hatch replaces the lowest Click Power pet if it is better.
16. Greedy player policy, gym: when no egg is worth buying, save for the gym fee, pay it, and fight. After 3 losses in a row the player is stuck.
17. Greedy player policy, stuck: rebirth if affordable. Otherwise buy the egg with the best expected battle gain per coin and replace the weakest battler. Each team change gives 3 more gym tries. After 25 such hatches in one stuck spell, or if that egg costs as much as the rebirth, save for the rebirth instead. Players only rebirth when stuck.
18. Rebirth k costs rebirth_first_cost x rebirth_cost_growth^(k-1). It resets coins, gym progress and paid fees. Pets and the index stay. Eggs of locked worlds cannot be bought.
19. Target check for 'rebirths at gym' uses the median player. Pacing checks use the median at 16.0 clicks per second.

Training uses configured hits/tick for the main cohort and 50% uptime for the slower cohort. Historical clicks/sec labels mean training hits/sec. Travel, idle time and offline income are excluded from this active-session baseline.
