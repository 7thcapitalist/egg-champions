- Failed submissions: 6 (27 total). These produced no model, so I did not count them as attempts.
  - 3 "Character limit exceeded": my first prompts were about 650 characters. Prompts up to about 500 characters were accepted.
  - 2 "Moderation failed", both on Zapeel 3 wordings. The first had "ready to strike", "red inside" and "bursting"; the second still had "electric eel", "belly", "flank" and "goofy". A plainer rewrite passed. The exact trigger is not identified.
  - 1 auto-mode safety classifier timeout on a generate_mesh call; an immediate retry worked.
- No "Too Many Requests" errors with 3 jobs running at once.
- Models from async jobs sometimes appeared at the workspace root before I had polled the job.

SUGGESTED RULE CHANGES

1. State a hard prompt limit of about 480 characters. The style rules plus the full quadruped boilerplate barely fit, so trim the boilerplate.
2. Moderation rejected the two Zapeel wordings above; when it does, plainer wording passed.
3. Features made of thin, leafy or soft material were never generated: the kelp mane (0 of 3), cloud wings (0 of 3) and wingtip spray (weak in 3 of 3). Either redesign those looks around chunky solid shapes (for example Kelpony with a few thick kelp fins) or plan to add them as separate meshes.
4. Painted markings on a set body color are ignored: yellow side stripes on the eel failed 3 of 3 and kept coming out as a pale belly. Ask for raised ridges or fins in the marking color, or fix it in a texture edit.
5. Size flyers by wingspan and give a wingspan target, not a height target.
6. "Coiled" always gives a flat ring like a pool float with a vertical neck. Try "S-shaped body rearing up" for serpents.
7. Words that helped against cute: "slim athletic", "wedge head", "angular angry brows", "slit eyes", "dragon-like head". "Not cute, not chibi" on its own did not.
8. Retries are not reliably better: attempt 1 was the best for Bubblecrab, Zapeel and Fuzzbolt. Two attempts per pet is probably enough before changing the design itself.
9. Scripts can rely on zeroed rotation for facing: every model faced -Z once its rotation was zeroed.
